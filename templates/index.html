Device Connection Guide & FAQ

Troubleshooting tips for iPhone, Android, Mac, and Windows connecting over Wi-Fi

Security

Why does this link use http:// instead of https://?

▼

You are 100% safe and private. NearBeam transfers files strictly over your local home Wi-Fi network directly between your devices:

  • No Internet / Cloud Involved: Transfers never leave your home Wi-Fi router. Nobody outside your Wi-Fi can see or access your files.
  • Why No HTTPS?: Official SSL/HTTPS certificates are issued only to public internet domains (like google.com). They cannot be issued for private local router IPs (like 192.168.x.x).
  • No False Warnings: Using a fake/self-signed HTTPS certificate triggers alarming red warning screens on iOS and Android ("Your connection is not private / Attackers might be trying to steal info"). Using clean HTTP avoids these scary false alarms.
iPhone & iPad

Chrome on iPhone is stuck loading or won't open

▼

Apple iOS restricts third-party browsers like Google Chrome from connecting to local Wi-Fi devices by default. Here is how to fix it in 5 seconds:

1
Enable Local Network Permission: Open iPhone Settings → scroll down to Chrome → turn Local Network ON (Green).
2
Always Include http://: If you type 192.168.1.11:5000, Chrome auto-switches to https:// which hangs. Type explicitly: http://192.168.1.11:5000
3
Best Solution: Use Safari: Safari is built into iOS and doesn't suffer from Chrome's restrictions. Open Safari and go to http://nearbeam.local:5000 or http://192.168.1.11:5000.
iOS / VPN

"This site cannot be reached" or Connection Timed Out

▼

If your phone cannot reach your PC at all, check these 3 common network blockers:

  • iCloud Private Relay: On iPhone, go to Settings → tap your Name / Apple ID → iCloud → Private Relay → Turn OFF temporarily. Private Relay reroutes your traffic through Apple servers, preventing access to your local Wi-Fi.
  • VPN Apps: If you have any VPN running on your phone or PC (e.g. 1.1.1.1, NordVPN, ExpressVPN), disconnect it while transferring files.
  • Different Wi-Fi / Cellular Data: Ensure your phone is connected to the exact same Wi-Fi SSID as your PC, not mobile cellular data (5G/LTE).
Android

Android phones: "Wi-Fi has no internet" prompt

▼

If your router has no active internet connection or slow connectivity, Android may display a prompt:

When Android asks "This Wi-Fi network has no internet access. Switch to mobile data?", always select "Stay connected to Wi-Fi". Otherwise, Android will route all web traffic through mobile 5G/4G, which cannot reach your PC.

Network

What address should I use to connect?

▼

NearBeam offers 3 convenient ways to connect:

  • Permanent Address (Bookmark this!): http://nearbeam.local:5000 — Even when your router changes your PC's IP address, mDNS keeps this URL working permanently on Safari, Mac, and iOS.
  • Direct IP: http://192.168.1.11:5000 — Works on all browsers and platforms without exception.
  • Subnet Auto-Finder: Visit /finder on any connected device. It sweeps your Wi-Fi subnet in 1 second and redirects you to the PC automatically!
Router Settings

Devices are on the same Wi-Fi but still cannot communicate

▼

Some Wi-Fi routers have a security feature called AP Isolation (or Client Isolation / Guest Wi-Fi):

  • Guest Wi-Fi: If your PC is on your main Wi-Fi and your phone is on "Guest Wi-Fi", the router isolates them. Connect both to the main network.
  • AP Isolation: Log in to your router's admin page (usually 192.168.1.1) and ensure "AP Isolation" / "Client Isolation" is turned Disabled.
